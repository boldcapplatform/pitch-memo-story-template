# The Taste Ledger

Twelve components of a company's taste. Excavate all twelve in Phase 1; anything unanswered goes to the gap list. These are not a section of the pitch — they are raw material distributed across chapters, and together they answer the question investors are actually asking at seed: *what does it feel like to think like this team?*

For each: the prompt to ask, and what a real answer sounds like.

1. **The Opinion** — What do you believe about your space that most competent people in it would argue with? *Real: a claim that divides a smart room. Fake: a mission statement everyone nods at.*

2. **The Tradeoff That Cost Us** — What choice hurt — money, time, a customer — and you'd make again? *Real: the cost is named in numbers. Fake: "we prioritize quality over speed."*

3. **The Feature We Killed** — What did you build or get begged for, and refuse? Who was angry? *Real: a name-attached refusal. Fake: a roadmap of what's coming instead.*

4. **Why This Taste Wins Now** — What is your market's reigning average, and what just changed that makes the average newly wrong? *Real: describes the default intimately before deviating from it. Fake: contrarianism without a map of the consensus.*

5. **How It Feels** — Which detail did you polish that nobody demanded? The empty state, the error message, the invoice? *Real: a detail from off the demo path. Fake: the demo path.*

6. **The Workaround We Refused** — Where was the cheap shortcut available — human-in-the-loop dressed as AI, the demo-friendly hack — and you took the expensive path? *Real: invisible in a demo, decisive in year two. Fake: any refusal that also happened to be easier.*

7. **The Hire We Passed On** — Who impressed everyone and didn't get hired? What seat stayed open because the bar is the bar? *Real: a specific pass with its cost in months. Fake: "we only hire A-players."*

8. **Our Promise** — What can a customer hold you to when the happy path breaks? *Real: a standard with teeth ("if you ever see us do X, we've lost the thread"). Fake: an SLA restated as values.*

9. **The People We Keep Close** — Who do you associate with — advisors, early users, peers — and what does that circle say about your standards? *Real: names whose presence is itself a filter. Fake: a logo wall of loose acquaintances.*

10. **What Influences Us** — Which builders, writers, products, or fields shape how you work? *Real: influences visible in the product's actual decisions. Fake: a bookshelf performed for the meeting.*

11. **Whose Ideologies We Align With** — Which schools of thought do you subscribe to — and, more revealing, which fashionable ones do you reject? *Real: an alignment with consequences ("we side with X, which is why we don't do Y"). Fake: alignment with whatever the room believes.*

12. **Our View of the Future** — Describe the world in five years if you're right. What exists, what's gone, what do people no longer tolerate? *Real: a concrete scene from that world. Fake: a market projection wearing a fortune-teller's hat.*

## Using the Ledger

- Components 1–8 are fingerprint material: they carry proof and belong in the pitch's spine.
- Components 9–12 are resonance material: they signal identity, and belong in conversation, the appendix, or the closing chapter — one or two woven in, never all four, never as a list.
- The ledger is also a diagnostic. A founder who fills 1–8 easily has lived their taste; a founder fluent only in 9–12 has curated it. Tell them which one they are.

## Where Each Component Lands

Every ledger answer has a home in the Story Deck chapters (`template.md`) and the presentation patterns (`presentation.md`). Route them; never leave them as a list:

| Ledger component | Chapter / section it feeds |
| --- | --- |
| 1. The Opinion | Ch 01 The Shift (the arguable belief) |
| 2. The Tradeoff That Cost Us | Ch 04 The Bets; Technical Appendix |
| 3. The Feature We Killed | Ch 04 The Bets or Ch 07 The Moat |
| 4. Why This Taste Wins Now | Ch 02 Why Now |
| 5. How It Feels | Ch 05 The Product — the Annotated Run |
| 6. The Workaround We Refused | Ch 07 The Moat; the demo's unhappy path in Ch 05 The Product |
| 7. The Hire We Passed On | Ch 09 Why Us — the Fingerprint Bios' team bar |
| 8. Our Promise | Ch 10 The Ask & the Standard (the non-negotiable) |
| 9. The People We Keep Close | Fingerprint Bios closing line |
| 10. What Influences Us | Conversation and appendix only |
| 11. Whose Ideologies We Align With | Ch 01 The Shift, if it carries a consequence; else conversation |
| 12. Our View of the Future | The spin's third world-state; Ch 10 The Ask & the Standard's horizon line |
